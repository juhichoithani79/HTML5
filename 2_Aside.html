2 countries of Asia

India – The Land of Diversity

India is a beautiful country in South Asia. Its capital is New Delhi. India is famous for its rich culture, traditions, festivals, and historical monuments. It is the world's largest country by population. The national flag of India has three colours: saffron, white, and green. India is known for its unity in diversity, delicious food, and beautiful landscapes. It is a country with a proud history and a bright future.

Japan – The Land of the Rising Sun

Japan is a beautiful country in East Asia. Its capital is Tokyo. Japan is famous for its advanced technology, beautiful cherry blossoms, and rich culture. It is known as the Land of the Rising Sun. Japan is also famous for sushi, anime, and Mount Fuji. The people of Japan are known for their discipline, hard work, and respect for others. Japan is a modern country with a rich history and traditions.
